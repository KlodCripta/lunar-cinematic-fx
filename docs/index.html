    Our Audio Enhancement Services

    32-Band EQ

    The Lunar Cinematic FX preset uses a finely tuned 32-band equalizer curve, carefully balanced to deliver a natural yet powerful sound. Each frequency range has been optimized to enhance low-end impact, vocal clarity, and overall depth for a richer cinematic listening experience.

    Stereo Tools

    The Stereo Tools module was used to carefully manage stereo width and phase alignment. By fine-tuning side and mid-level balance, the preset delivers a wider and more natural stereo image without losing focus in the center channel. This enhances immersion and ensures a consistent listening experience across different types of audio content.

    Bass Emphasis

    This stage focuses on delivering a powerful yet controlled low-end. Carefully tuned harmonics and thresholds enhance bass depth and impact without muddying the overall mix.

    Loudness Bass

    The Loudness Bass module has been carefully tuned to give more presence and body to the low frequencies without sacrificing overall clarity. By balancing perceived volume and dynamics, the bass becomes fuller and punchier—ideal for movies and music that demand impact and depth.

    Limiter

    The Limiter ensures that the overall output remains clean and distortion-free, even during the most intense moments of a soundtrack. It has been fine-tuned to control sudden peaks without overly compressing the dynamics, maintaining a natural and immersive sound.

    Overall Enhancement

    This stage fine-tunes the overall audio output for headphone listening, ensuring that every element—from low-end impact to high-frequency clarity—works together harmoniously. It maximizes listening enjoyment, making sound richer, more dynamic, and cinematic.
